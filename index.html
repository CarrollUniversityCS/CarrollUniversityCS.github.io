    Collecting

    How quickly Wisconsin's nonfederal .gov websites patch critical vulnerabilities, starting with PHP2Shell (CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619). Passive measurement: one ordinary homepage request per site per snapshot, aggregate results only, and an opt-out for any site.
